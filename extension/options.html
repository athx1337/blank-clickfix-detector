BLANK checks webpage instructions for deceptive requests to paste and execute commands. Everything runs in your browser. No account, server, or pairing required.

  1. Open an HTTP or HTTPS webpage.
  2. Click BLANK in the browser toolbar, then Scan this page.
  3. For background checks, turn on Automatic scans on this site in the popup and grant website access.

    Cloud AI · optional

    Local scans work without a key. To request a cloud second opinion, enter your own provider key. No shared key or account is supplied by BLANK.

    Keys are kept in trusted browser session storage, not synchronized or saved across browser restarts. This is not an encrypted secret vault. Browser access can also be revoked in extension settings.

    Each cloud request needs a passage preview and confirmation. Gemini’s free tier may use submitted text to improve products. Groq has retention exceptions and account controls. Quotas and any charges depend on your own provider account; BLANK does not activate billing.

    Local by default

    Automatic and normal manual scans stay on your device. Only passages you explicitly select and confirm are sent directly to your chosen cloud provider. No analytics or scan history is kept. The latest local result per tab stays in trusted session storage until navigation, tab close, or browser restart.

    Automatic access applies to a hostname and may span ports. Disable a site in the popup or revoke access through the browser’s extension settings.

    BLANK warns; it does not block commands or guarantee protection. English and Windows-oriented instructions, visible main document only. Images, nested frames, and shadow roots are outside coverage.

    The bundled classifier is experimental and trained on 64 synthetic examples. Review evidence carefully; research validation on representative data remains outstanding.
